Our Origin & Ethos

The Art of Mindful Solitude

"Coffee is not merely an awakening ritual; it is an architectural symphony of earth, craft, and contemplation."

Founded with a reverent homage to historic Milanese espresso sanctuaries and Parisian salons de thé, Caffè Veloura was designed for those who appreciate the grace of unhurried craftsmanship. We roast in micro-batches, calibrating moisture, barometric pressure, and water mineral profiles to reveal the untamed floral nuances of high-altitude beans.

Ethically Sourced Beans

Direct-trade alliances with boutique generational growers across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala ensure equitable compensation and sustainable terroir stewardship.

Crafted with Precision

Every extraction is dialed in three times daily by Master Baristas, calibrated by gram, temperature curve, and extraction pressure.

Moments Made Special

Served in handcrafted Italian porcelain with curated acoustic jazz, creating an intimate sanctuary away from the hurried world.

V60 & Chemex Single-Origin Ritual

Precision Pour-Over Extraction

A delicate manual extraction that unveils the subtle floral notes, delicate citrus brightness, and distinct terroir of our Ethiopian and Geisha beans. Water is poured in concentric spirals at a precise 92°C with a spiral flow rate.

Selected Terroir Single-Origin Yirgacheffe & Gesha Village
Water Ratio 1:16 Coffee-to-Water
Brew Temperature 92°C / 197.6°F
Extraction Time 3 min 15 sec
